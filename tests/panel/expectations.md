# Persona panel expectations (written by Claude, before any run of the panel)

Written 2 October 2026 from the one-line descriptions and rating lists in
tests/panel/panel_profiles.py (50 people) and panel_profiles_extra.py (10 people,
added by the same author to fill gaps: nobody improvised, only four wide, nobody at 70+).
No app output on these 60 people had been seen when this was written. Do not edit this file after it is committed. If an expectation
turns out to be wrong, say so in the notes; do not change it here.

THE FOUR QUESTIONS (one bit each, as the app defines them)
  B  breadth       HIGH = what the person kept is spread over several unrelated fields.
                   LOW = concentrated in one field.
  P  process       HIGH = planned, repeatable, to a specification.
                   LOW = improvised, intuitive, found as it goes.
  S  sociability   HIGH = the working practice is done with other people.
                   LOW = done alone.
  F  form          HIGH = open to interpretation (the output means different things to different
                   people). LOW = literal, functional or fixed.
EITHER means a reasonable person could argue both ways from the description. EITHER never counts as
agreement. About a third of the cells below are EITHER on purpose: where the description does not
decide, this file does not pretend it does.

HOW TO PARSE: one line per person, fields separated by " | ", in this order:
  key | B=<HIGH/LOW/EITHER> | P=... | S=... | F=... | reason
A person with "NONE" in every field is expected to receive no genus at all.
