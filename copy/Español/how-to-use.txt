Holotype takes what you already know and shows you what's near it, and what's genuinely far from it, across {0} art forms. You rate what you've done, Holotype ranks the rest by how new it would be to you, and the Herbarium turns your ratings into a portrait of how you tend to work.
Valora las actividades creativas que has probado entre {0} disciplinas. Holotipo te propone qué explorar después, desde habilidades cercanas a las tuyas hasta algo nuevo. En el Herbario encontrarás una flor, una planta y una lectura de tus valoraciones.

The <b>Garden</b> is where you rate: every art form is a tile, with your rated ones collected at the top. The <b>Herbarium</b> is where what you have grown gets pressed, labeled and kept: your holotype, your growth habit, your rated and unexplored lists, and the PDF export, all built live from your ratings. Switch with the tabs in the top bar; nothing is lost when you do.
En el <b>Jardín</b>, cada disciplina tiene una tarjeta y un control para valorarla. Las que ya valoraste aparecen arriba. El <b>Herbario</b> muestra tu flor, planta, perfil y listas, que puedes guardar en PDF. Cambia de vista con las pestañas de la barra superior.

Each art form is drawn as a flower from its own profile. Pointed outer petals show how it is made and shared, rounded middle bars the field it serves, and inner dots the senses it uses. A longer shape is a stronger trait, so similar practices grow similar-looking flowers. The ink comes from its category, and since categories share four inks, the shape tells you more than the color.
Cada flor representa el perfil de una disciplina: los pétalos exteriores describen cómo se hace y se comparte, los centrales sus campos y los puntos interiores sus sentidos. Las formas más largas indican rasgos más marcados. Las prácticas parecidas tienen flores parecidas. Varias categorías comparten colores, así que fíjate también en la forma.

Some art forms contain more specific ones. A small <b>+4</b> beside a name means four more specific forms sit inside it (Dance holds Flamenco, Capoeira, Chinese Opera and Pole Dance). Each of those has its own tile, with the larger form named in small type above its name. Open either side and you see the other: a larger form lists the forms inside it, and a specific form shows a <b>Part of a larger art form</b> section naming its parent, plus the other forms under the same parent. They are ordinary art forms, rated separately, so rate whichever level matches what you actually do.
Un <b>+4</b> junto a un nombre indica que incluye cuatro disciplinas más específicas, cada una con su tarjeta. Danza, por ejemplo, incluye Flamenco, Capoeira, Ópera china y Pole dance. Abre una tarjeta para ver esas relaciones. Las disciplinas específicas indican su categoría superior en <b>Parte de una disciplina más amplia</b>. Valora la práctica general, la específica o ambas según tu experiencia.

Use the slider on a tile to rate anything you've actually done, from 1 to 10. There's no need to rate everything, and most people should use the low end of the scale far more than the high one: a 10 is a claim only someone doing it for a living should honestly make.
Usa los controles para valorar lo que has hecho del 1 al 10. Deja sin valorar lo que no has probado. Un primer intento cuenta como 1; un 10 indica dominio a nivel profesional.

Once you've rated something, every other tile grows a ring showing how new it would be to you: the fuller the ring, the newer the ground. The number inside is the novelty score, from 1 to 100: a rough answer to "how much of this would I already recognize?" A higher rating of yours counts for more, and knowing several related things adds up to count for more than any one of them alone.
Tras tu primera valoración, las tarjetas sin valorar muestran una puntuación de novedad del 1 al 100. Un anillo más lleno y un número mayor indican que la práctica está más lejos de lo que conoces. Las valoraciones altas y la experiencia en varias disciplinas relacionadas cuentan más para calcular la cercanía.

The sort menu in the top bar orders the art forms you have not rated. It opens on <b>Most common first</b>: the art forms the most people have tried at some point, an estimated share of adults shown beside each one in the Herbarium list (a filled dot is a published survey figure, a hollow ring is an estimate). <b>Less common first</b> reverses it. <b>Closest to you first</b> and <b>Furthest from you first</b> need at least one rating to mean anything: closest leads with what is most like the things you rated, furthest is there if you would rather be surprised than be helped. <b>A to Z</b>, <b>By category</b> (grouped by field, nearest first within each) and <b>By tag</b> (grouped by your own color tags, so it needs some) complete the list. The Herbarium's "Not yet explored" list follows the same choice. Search by name at any time.
El menú empieza en <b>Más comunes primero</b>; <b>Menos comunes primero</b> invierte el orden. <b>Más cercanas a ti primero</b> y <b>Más lejanas a ti primero</b> usan tus valoraciones. También puedes ordenar <b>De A a Z</b>, <b>Por categoría</b> o <b>Por etiqueta</b>. El Herbario sigue el mismo orden. Sus cifras de participación estiman cuántos adultos han probado una práctica: un punto lleno indica un dato de encuesta y un círculo vacío una estimación. Busca una disciplina por nombre en el campo de búsqueda.

Filters narrow the catalogue by <b>category</b>, by how <b>new</b> something is to you (Familiar, In between, New ground), by which <b>half</b> you want to see (My garden, Not yet rated), and they hold the <b>reach</b> slider: turned up, everything you know counts for more, so more of it reads as familiar and the closest-first list tightens around your own field; turned down, only your nearest neighbours get credit and the rest of the catalog stays new ground.
Filtra por <b>categoría</b>, cercanía (Familiar, Intermedio o Terreno nuevo), o por si la disciplina está en <b>Mi jardín</b> o <b>Sin valorar</b>. El control de <b>alcance</b> ajusta cuánto cuenta tu experiencia: súbelo para dar más peso a las habilidades relacionadas o bájalo para considerar familiares solo las más cercanas.

All of these follow you across the app, not just the grid. Whatever you have filtered down to is what the <b>Herbarium page</b> lists and what the <b>PDF</b> contains, so a list you narrowed to five art forms exports as five rows rather than the whole catalog. Both halves are shown by default; switch one off and it leaves the page and the export too. When a filter is holding something back, the list heading says so, as "12 of {0}", so a short list is never mistaken for the whole thing. <b>Clear all</b> puts everything back.
Los filtros se aplican al Jardín, a la <b>página del Herbario</b> y al <b>PDF</b>. Si reduces una lista a cinco disciplinas, el archivo incluirá esas cinco. El encabezado indica cuántas se ven, por ejemplo «12 de {0}». Las disciplinas valoradas y sin valorar aparecen de forma predeterminada; <b>Limpiar todo</b> vuelve a mostrarlas.

The Herbarium's lists open <b>condensed</b>: stacked full width and split into three columns each, which fits most of the catalogue on one screen. The <b>Comfortable spacing</b> button loosens them into two side-by-side lists, what you rated on the left and what you have not on the right. That choice is for reading only — it is not saved to your session and does not change the PDF.
Las listas del Herbario empiezan en formato <b>compacto</b>, con tres columnas por lista en pantallas anchas. Elige <b>Espaciado cómodo</b> para ver dos listas lado a lado con más espacio entre las entradas. Esta opción de lectura no se guarda en la sesión ni cambia el PDF.

Open the palette icon to give any trait a color: category, cost, space, learning curve, self-taught friendly, or <b>distance from your taste</b> (a range from 0, very familiar, to 100, very new; it appears once you've rated something). Matching tiles get a small colored mark, several tags can stack, and in your Herbarium they show only on art forms you haven't rated yet. You start with none.
Usa el icono de la paleta para marcar con colores la categoría, el costo, el espacio, la curva de aprendizaje, la facilidad para aprender por tu cuenta o la <b>distancia respecto a tus gustos</b>. La distancia va de 0 (familiar) a 100 (nuevo) y aparece tras tu primera valoración. Una tarjeta puede llevar varias marcas. En el Herbario solo se muestran en disciplinas sin valorar. Añade las etiquetas que te sirvan.

The page is your <b>holotype</b>. The flower at the top is a fingerprint of your ratings, inked by the category you lean into most. Below it, your <b>growth habit</b> names how you tend to work, as a plant. The big italic word is your <b>genus</b>, drawn as its own plant and given a tribe name. Beneath that, written the way botanists write a name, sit up to two short Latin notes: <i>var.</i> (variety) for how you handle chance, and <i>cf.</i> (compare) for up to two fields your ratings clearly favor, each spelled out in plain words just below. The five bars show where you sit on each axis compared with other people. With fewer than four ratings, or while nothing you rated is above a first try (3 or higher), you get a provisional genus only, with no variety or leans yet. The plant's shape and its colors follow your ratings, so both keep moving as you rate: the plant is inked with the same three colors as your holotype, with the one you lean into most leading. What stays put is the small companion beside the plant and the exact tone of the colors: they are chosen once for you and saved in your session file.
Tu <b>holotipo</b> es la flor dibujada a partir de tus valoraciones. Tu <b>hábito de crecimiento</b> describe cómo sueles trabajar y se representa con una planta. El <b>género</b> es el nombre grande en cursiva. Debajo aparecen <i>var.</i> (variedad), por tu relación con el azar, y <i>cf.</i> (comparar), por hasta dos campos que destacan en tus valoraciones. Las barras te comparan con perfiles de referencia simulados. Con menos de cuatro valoraciones, o ninguna de 3 o más, la lectura es provisional y aún no incluye variedad ni notas de campo. La forma y los colores cambian con tus valoraciones; el pequeño acompañante y los tonos se conservan en tu sesión.

Botanical terms, and what they mean in biology as well as in Holotype, are explained in the <b>Glossary</b> tab of this window.
La pestaña <b>Glosario</b> de esta ventana explica los términos botánicos y su significado tanto en Holotipo como en biología.

On the Herbarium page, choose which sections to include and press Save as PDF; it uses your browser's own print dialog and nothing is uploaded. The sheet contains exactly the art forms your filters currently allow, and the subheading says how many of the catalogue it lists. Include the <b>This session's filters</b> section to have the sheet record which filters, sort and search produced it, so a shortened sheet can be explained later. You can also save your session as a small file (ratings, name, tags, filters, sort, and your plant's colors and companion) and load it in a later visit to pick up where you left off. Sessions saved in earlier versions still load. If an art form has since been renamed it is matched automatically, and if it was split or removed your rating is set aside and listed for you to place again, never silently dropped; art forms added since are simply unrated until you find and rate them. The circular arrow in the top bar wipes ratings, tags, name and plant so the next person on the same device starts fresh (after a confirmation).
En el Herbario, elige las secciones y pulsa Guardar como PDF para abrir el diálogo de impresión del navegador. El PDF respeta los filtros actuales. Incluye <b>Filtros de esta sesión</b> para recordar cómo preparaste la lista. Guarda un archivo de sesión con tus valoraciones, nombre, etiquetas, filtros, orden, idioma y planta; después cárgalo para continuar en otro dispositivo. Los archivos antiguos siguen funcionando: las disciplinas renombradas se reconocen y las que faltan se conservan aparte para que las revises. La flecha circular borra tus valoraciones, etiquetas, nombre y planta, y devuelve la aplicación al inglés tras confirmar.
